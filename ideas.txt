training ideas:
	seek target (moving or stationary or last seen location)
	run away from target
	hide from target
	stay in shadows
	stay in light
game idea:
world filled with resources. Slaves farm resources and carry resources. Creatures attack miners
	Soldiers protect miners and hunt creatures. Commanders give orders to squads of soldiers
	generals give orders to commanders. generals try to get as much resource as they can
	players manage squad either in a faction or not
		if in a faction players must give some of their resource
	squad members can get enslaved and become miners
subclasses:
	slaves: miners, carriers
	creatures: wolves, dragons
	soldiers: fighter, tank, mage, healer, ranger
	commanders:
		greedy: max resource and loot
		leader: preserve squad
		hero: save slaves
		slavemaster: recruit slaves
		ambitious: recruit soldiers and become general
	generals: different personality types and goals?
		greedy: wants to maximize total resource
		savvy: wants to max resource rate
		aggressive: wants to conquer other factions
		prideful: wants resource in order to build statues
		chaos: just wants to make chaos in the world
		
game ai systems:
	use reinforcement learning and traditional procedural ai
	miners get basic rules or simplest training
	how to train multiple models?
		keep every model as it is and only train one model at a time
		start with procedural ai for everyone except first class trained?
		ie train soldiers against the same dragon then train the dragon against the latest ai model of soldiers
		then train the soldiers again on the improved dragon and so on and so on
	training scenarios:
		miners: wander around until they find resource. then mine some and bring it back home
			at the same time talk to other miners and let them know approx where the resource is
		soldiers: 
			fight soldiers. if one dies -10 reward and tp it far away
			fight dumb swarm of enemies.
