training ideas:
	seek target (moving or stationary or last seen location)
	run away from target
	hide from target
	stay in shadows
	stay in light
game idea:
world filled with resources. Slaves farm resources and carry resources. Creatures attack miners
	Soldiers protect miners and hunt creatures. Commanders give orders to squads of soldiers
	generals give orders to commanders. generals try to get as much resource as they can
	players manage squad either in a faction or not
		if in a faction players must give some of their resource
	squad members can get enslaved and become miners
subclasses:
	slaves: miners, carriers
	creatures: wolves, dragons
	soldiers: fighter, tank, mage, healer, ranger
	commanders:
		greedy: max resource and loot
		leader: preserve squad
		hero: save slaves
		slavemaster: recruit slaves
		ambitious: recruit soldiers and become general
	generals: different personality types and goals?
		greedy: wants to maximize total resource
		savvy: wants to max resource rate
		aggressive: wants to conquer other factions
		prideful: wants resource in order to build statues
		chaos: just wants to make chaos in the world

grok ideas:
	train commands using one-hot system: array with only 1 in position representing command.
		modify reward function to reward following current command
		can blend commands. recommend max two blended commands and might require training blended commands. reward function blends rewards too
	Rule of thumb: if a human can learn the task in under a minute of watching, and you can write a dense reward, it belongs on your GPU.
	don't train a commander to order soldiers who are still learning themselves. Only train commanders to order completed soldier neural nets
	generals need too much time to see the reward. therefore dont use RL or only let human players be generals, or train generals on abstraction
	Cover rays = geometry-aware ray features, or tagged hits; not mandatory as a separate system.
		Recommendation for a Kenshi clone: authored cover features + let the policy learn the timing. Pure discovery is a research flex, not a production shortcut.
	First skills: under a minute, ideally under 30 seconds.
	Commanders: train against finished (or scripted) soldiers.
